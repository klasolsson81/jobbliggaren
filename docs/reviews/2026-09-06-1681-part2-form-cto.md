## Reasoning, condensed

**On the form.** Form 1 wins seven of the eight measured statement points and both composed rows.
Form 2's single win (N=20 counts, at-bound: 15,8 vs 25,0) does not survive composition, because the
same cohort makes its id set the most expensive point in the study (41,7 vs 27,5). The verdict is
insensitive to the report's Result-4 defect: under every reading — refused-only, at-the-gate,
realistic, or N=1 — Form 1 either wins or loses by 9,2 ms (3,1 % of one page budget) in a state the
register distribution puts at 2 criteria in 627 and 0 in 101 097. *"A form whose best case is 3 % of
a budget in a non-ordinary state, bought by losing an order of magnitude in the ordinary one, is not
a candidate."*

Form 2 also inherits ADR 0139 alternative 2's failure-semantics objection unchanged: *"En fan-out
felar minst N gånger synligt; batchen hänger en gång, tyst."*

**On one shape vs two.** Had Form 2 won the list, two shapes would have had to be weighed against
this port's standing discipline (*"Two methods, one predicate authority… Co-locating the predicate is
the drift defense"*) and ADR 0139's *"Båda ytorna läser samma källa"*. It does not win it, so the
SRP-clean answer and the fast answer coincide and no trade-off is being accepted.

**On the bound.** Form 1 *is* the shape the bound was derived against, so the re-derivation trigger
is not met. The CTO nonetheless re-tested both anchors under part 2's composition (two statements per
criterion where part 1 assumed one): N=1 lands at 2,7x/9,6x the baseline (in class, where part 1's
in/out boundary was 6,2x/17,6x); the realistic block is 8,6 % of 300 ms against part 1's own 9,9 %;
and at 5x the bound one block would be ~56 % of the page. **Both anchors still land on 1 000, the
second more firmly than before.** `MaxPerCriterion` stays 1000 and **no docblock number moves** —
`CompanyWatchCriterionMember.cs` keeps the measurements in one home, and adding part-2 figures there
would re-create the second-home defect code-reviewer already removed once.

⚠ The inheritance covers `members ⋈ job_ads` and **stops there**. Part 1 measured no grading at all,
so nothing about the bound speaks to the fan-in.

**On the fan-in.** Form-invariant — both forms hand the same ids to the same grader. What is new is
that part 2 puts grading of criterion ad sets on `MeListRead` / `/oversikt` (300 ms, ADR 0045 class
(a)) for the first time, at up to 20x the only fan-in size ever measured in production shape. Three
reasons the floor is not enough: the dominant term (`GradeRankExpression`) is unmeasured; the series
is non-monotone so no extrapolation exists; and Result 4's budget claim is not established for the
composed surface. Batching is ordered on the twin's own precedent —
`ListCompanyWatchesQueryHandler` grades in one `CountPerUserByEmployerAsync` call over the union of
every watched org.nr — and on measurement: one call at 4 040 ids costs 4,5 ms against 8,4 ms for one
call at 2 000. *"Batching the grading is the twin's own shape and measures cheaper; batching the
statement measures dearer. Two different questions, both answered on measurement rather than on a
preference for batching."*

**On the paged browse.** The detail page's number links to that page. A materialised count over a
register-backed list is *"one authority producing two measurements at two instants over the same
question"* — verbatim the defect the resolver exists to close. It would also leave one surface
reading two sources internally (the `?visa=matchande` arm materialised, the unfiltered arm not). It
is additionally **cheaper**: same shape, N=1, one page of 20.

Moving set: **`CountActiveAdsAsync`, `ListActiveAdIdsAsync`, `BrowseAdIdsAsync`.** Verified by
call-site sweep that all three serve only saved, owner-loaded criteria (resolver ×2,
`BrowseCriterionAdsQueryHandler` ×1), so re-keying them from `CompanyWatchCriteriaSpec` to
`CompanyWatchCriterionId` orphans nothing.

Two consequences the implementation may not skip:

- **The port grows a second key type, and that is correct, not a smell.** The company half answers
  *"what does this PREDICATE match, live"* and must stay spec-keyed for the picker's unsaved preview;
  the ad half answers *"what did we MATERIALISE for this SAVED criterion"*. Record the reasoning
  beside the existing *"Takes the Domain VO, not a criterion id"* paragraph, or the next reader reads
  a contradiction.
- **The closed hierarchy gains a FOURTH arm.** "Never materialised" is ignorance; `SetTooLarge` is a
  determinate refusal. Collapsing them is the dishonest-zero class one level up, and ADR 0139
  alternative 4 already rejected null-carries-three-meanings for exactly this reason. Extend
  `CriterionMatchingAds` and give the ad magnitude the same three-state treatment. New copy is owed
  on the ads route, which today renders only `tooBroad` and `notAssessed`; an empty ad list in the
  unknown state would be the false zero.

**On the company reads.** No live alternative exists: the member table stores only
`(criterion_id, organization_number)` while the browse projects `company_name`,
`sate_kommun_code`, `sate_kommun_name`, `sni_codes`. Serving it from members would mean copying
register columns into the derived table — an ADR 0087 D2/D3 snapshot and a larger derived-PII surface
arguing against the Art. 5(1)(c) minimisation the breadth gate carries. `CountMatchingCompaniesAsync`
additionally has an unsaved-criterion caller (`PreviewCriterionMatchMagnitudeQueryHandler`).

*Rejected — moving only the company magnitude to `MemberCount`:* wrong twice. `MemberCount` is 0
under `TooBroad` by design, so the refusal state would carry no number; and the paged company list
must stay register-backed regardless, so the headline and its own destination would then read
different sources.

*The co-render is honest.* Above the gate the page says "10 000+ företag matchar" and "för bred" for
the ads — two questions, two ceilings, the port's own doctrine. **The company magnitude is the
evidence for the refusal**; suppressing it would leave the refusal unexplained.

⚠ **Copy condition (binding):** the "för bred" line must name **breadth** as the reason, so the two
numbers read as one coherent statement, never as one number failing beside another succeeding. The
existing `ads.matchingTooBroad` vocabulary already does this; the new ad-magnitude refusal must match
it rather than mint a second phrasing.

⚠ **Named residual, to be recorded and not left implicit:** the company magnitude is resolved live
while the members are materialised, so the two rest on two resolutions of the same register predicate
at two instants. ADR 0139's *"Det kostar ingen färskhet"* is about the ad numbers in isolation and
does not reach this composition. Write the residual into the ADR or the port docblock.

## Findings against the measurement report

Both are **composition** defects, not measurement defects, and neither changes the form choice.
Both were verified against the source by the session and **corrected in the report** the same day.

- **A — the at-bound row is internally inconsistent and cannot describe a reachable state.** It
  charges the id-set statement *and* zeroes the fan-in with "— (all refused)". What zeroes the fan-in
  is the resolver's guard order (magnitude → `> MaxSetSize` → `SetTooLarge`), at which point
  `ListActiveAdIdsAsync` is never called. At 2 004 ads every criterion is refused, so the id column
  should be 0 too. *(The id-set measurement itself stays meaningful: a criterion at exactly 2 000
  passes the gate and the port then reads 2 001 rows.)*
- **B — the block is labelled an UPPER BOUND while one of its three columns is a FLOOR.** A sum of an
  upper bound and a floor is neither. So *"Both forms fit"* is established for the **statement
  halves** and **not** for the composed surface including grading — which is precisely what
  sub-question 3 turns on.

## Trade-offs accepted (verbatim from the verdict)

- **Up to ~40 statements plus one grading call per list request**, against the twin's 2–3. Accepted:
  it is what the measurement priced, and ADR 0139 alternative 2 already ruled a visible fan-out beats
  a silent batch on failure semantics. **The CTO declines to merge the count and the id-set statements
  per criterion** — the resolver's guard order refuses cheaply *before* the expensive query, and
  merging them would spend the id-set cost on every refused criterion.
- **The port carries two key types.** Costs one docblock paragraph.
- **The closed hierarchy grows to four arms.** Honesty is why it is closed at all; a fourth honest
  state is the design working, not eroding.
- **`ListCompanyWatchCriteriaQuery` gets heavier for every existing consumer**, including the detail
  page, which calls `getCompanyWatchCriteria()` only to resolve a label and will now pay the whole
  fan-out for it (~26 ms realistic). Accepted for part 2: that route is not on the 300 ms budget.
  **Do not solve it with an opt-in flag** — ADR 0139 alternative 4 closed that on two independent
  grounds. If it proves to matter, a cheaper label source is a follow-up issue, not part-2 scope.
