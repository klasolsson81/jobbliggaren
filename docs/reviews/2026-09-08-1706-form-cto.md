## D1 — Decision **[BOUND]**

**The bound MOVES, and it moves only on the re-derivation protocolled below. It does not move in
this PR. `MaxPerCriterion` stays 1000 today — not because 1000 is right, but because every value
available today would be CHOSEN, and #1681's acceptance and the constant's own docblock forbid
that.**

Four things follow, and all four are binding:

1. **#1706 is NOT closed by copy.** Its acceptance offers an escape — *"eller så finns ett skrivet,
   mätt skäl till varför den inte kan det"* — and **measurement has closed that escape**: §4 of the
   new report shows admitting `{62100} x {1480}` costs the selection **nothing** (5,88 → 6,18 ms
   p50 across the band), and §3 shows the read curve smooth and sub-linear through 1 000–3 000 with
   **no plan change anywhere in it**. There is no measured reason why it cannot. An honest-refusal
   answer here would be Regel 4's disguised skip.
2. **The three loads no longer point the same way, and that is the finding.** The product load has
   **inverted**: the gate now refuses the narrowest criterion the product can express, 3 of 5 dev
   criteria, and 57,2 % of Stockholm's company mass. A gate whose product load argues against its
   own value is a defect, not a trade-off.
3. **The bound's remaining loads are read cost and Art. 5(1)(c) — the product-honesty load moves off
   this constant.** `CriterionMatchingAdSetResolver.MaxSetSize` = 2 000 ads already carries "this
   watch is too broad to be useful", derived from the destination's pagination, on the axis the user
   actually experiences. The company-side constant refuses on an axis the user never sees. After the
   re-derivation it is a **cost and storage ceiling**, and its product-visible refusal is a
   consequence to be minimised rather than a feature to be defended.
4. **Anchor 1 is RETIRED as a ratio, and buffers may NOT replace it.** See below — this is the
   architectural judgement §6 of the report asked me for.

### Motivering mot principer

- **A constant is meaningless without its derivation (Martin 2017, kap. 7 — SRP: one authority per
  knowledge piece).** `MaxPerCriterion` is not a number, it is the *output of a protocol*. Moving it
  by reading a sweep table and picking a row replaces the protocol with taste — which is the one
  thing `CompanyWatchMaterialisationOptionsTests:65`'s literal pin exists to force a confrontation
  over (*"a nudge is invisible"*). I decline to make that pin's comment false.
- **KISS / YAGNI do not license a shortcut here.** Every quick form on the table buys product relief
  with a claim we cannot stand behind, and Regel 1 puts principle above tempo precisely when the
  cheap answer is available.
- **ADR 0120 "exakt-eller-frånvarande" + #859 (*a rendered magnitude must be true*)** kill partial
  materialisation outright — below.
- **An acceptance is at a size.** Klas-beviljande 4 accepted **316,7 / 381,5 ms** on this exact
  handler at **this** bound. Raising the bound moves that figure by an unmeasured amount. Riding a
  raise in on his old grant would be widening a grant he did not give.
- **Ford/Parsons/Kua 2017 (fitness functions):** the correct response to "the measurement that
  governed this constant was taken against the wrong weighting" is to re-run the instrument, not to
  argue past it.

### Avvisade alternativ

**A — raise it now to a value from the sweep (2 000 / 2 500 / 3 000). Rejected, and this is the
one that would have been easy.** Anchor 1 is not re-derivable in its stated metric (§3b: the
denominator itself spreads 1,5x between runs of the same fixture, so the same point reads 7,2x or
13,6x depending on which baseline divides it — a ratio against an unstable sub-millisecond
denominator is a warm singleton wearing a p95's clothes, and `2026-09-04-1559-perf-test-writer.md`
already forbids a verdict on singletons). Anchor 2's real home is the composed real-schema
measurement Klas-beviljande 4 rests on, and **it has not been re-taken at any candidate value**.
Picking a row from a sweep whose own author labels the x20 column *"a shape, not a verdict"* is
choosing a number.

**B — buffers stand in for anchor 1. Rejected on its own arithmetic, and this is decisive.** Apply
anchor 1's own in/out boundary (6,2x in, 17,6x out) to the buffer series: 10 000 members is
**5,4x** — comfortably "in class" — while the ms series puts the same point at **9,3x the cost of
1 000** and 226 % of the page budget at N=20. Buffers grow 3,3x where ms grows 9,3x over the same
span, so they **systematically understate** the growth this anchor exists to catch. Substituting
them would be selecting the instrument that flatters the desired direction. Buffers remain a valid
*comparative* metric between two statement shapes on a noisy host (which is what
`2026-09-06-1681-plan-probe-measurement.md` used them for); they cannot carry a *threshold*.

**C — partial materialisation with a saturated "1 000+". Rejected hardest of all.** The wire
already carrying `saturated` and `formatMagnitude` already rendering "+" makes this look free, and
it is not. (i) `SelectCandidatesAsync` has **no `ORDER BY`, deliberately and documented** — the
prefix is nondeterministic, so the same criterion would render different ad numbers on different
runs with no register change. (ii) The store's own docblock already rules on it: a truncated set
yields *"a FLOOR wearing a magnitude's clothes, and unlike a saturating count it would have no true
reading at all."* (iii) `formatMagnitude`'s contract is saturation **at a stated product ceiling** —
"10 000+" means *we stopped counting at 10 000*. "18+" over an arbitrary thousand rows means
nothing, and it would corrupt the matching number and the paged destination with it.

**D — a narrowing affordance in the picker as the fix. Rejected as a fix (kept as copy, below).**
`{62100} x {1480}` is one SNI leaf in one kommun: **there is nothing to narrow.** Advice that
cannot help is the exact fault this codebase already names in `criterion-ad-lines.tsx`
(*"inviting the user to edit it would be advice that cannot help"*).

**E — a member-count-invariant read shape (the measured `JOIN members` form). Rejected.** It is
nearly flat in member count (6,02 ms at 100, 9,16 at 10 000, 46,28 ungated), so it looks like it
dissolves the bound. It does not: at `MaxPerUser` = 20 it is 183–920 ms composed, and in the
1 000–3 000 band it is *worse* than the delivered shape. A hybrid dispatch would also mint a second
plan (against Klas-beviljande 4 condition 1's named EXPLAIN pin) and a second chosen threshold.

**F — materialise only ad-bearing companies (~0,93 % of the register). Rejected, and recorded
because it is the obvious idea and will be proposed again.** It looks strictly better on all three
loads and changes no ad number *at materialisation time*. But membership is materialised and
`job_ads` is live: a company quiet at materialisation and posting an hour later would be **missing
from the member set and its new ad uncounted** — a systematic silent miss of exactly the ads this
feature exists to surface. `CompanyWatchCriteriaSpec` calls a silent miss *"this product's cardinal
sin"*.

### Trade-offs accepterade

- **No product relief in this PR for an `mvp` P1 defect Klas hit personally.** Accepted, and it is
  the real cost of this ruling. Mitigated by the fact that the missing measurement is **one known
  protocol that has been run once already** (2026-09-07), not open research — this is sequencing,
  not refusal.
- **`MaxPerCriterion` stays at a value I have just called measurably wrong on one of its three
  loads.** Accepted: shipping a wrong-but-derived constant for one more PR is recoverable; shipping
  a chosen one destroys the rule that makes every future move honest.
- **If the derivation lands at or above 2 000, three EXPLAIN pins fail and `SeededRows` must grow.**
  Accepted, and stated in the opposite direction as a rule: **a test fixture is never an input to a
  derived product constant.** If the protocol lands on 2 500, the fixture grows to fit it; the
  constant does not shrink to fit the fixture.

## The re-derivation protocol **[BOUND]** — what #1706's next PR must run

**P1. Anchor 1 is retired and subsumed, not re-metricated.** Its function was to protect ADR 0139's
`MeListRead` conclusion (*"the bucket decision and `MaxPerCriterion` are ONE decision"*). That
conclusion is about the **composed** read, which P2 measures directly in absolute ms. A synthetic
per-statement ratio was always a proxy for it; the proxy has lost its instrument and the direct
measurement has not.

**P2. Anchor 2 is re-run in its real home**, i.e. the protocol of
`docs/reviews/2026-09-07-1681-part2-fanin-and-corpus-measurement.md` — real schema, real DI graph,
p95 over at least 40 runs — at each candidate member bound. **The state definitions are re-used
verbatim from that report. Inventing a new state for this derivation is forbidden**, because
choosing the state is how you choose the answer.

**P3. The acceptance test, and it is deliberately not "never widen anything".**
- **Binding ceiling:** the **everyday** composed state must stay inside ADR 0045 class (a)'s
  **300 ms p95**. That is where the budget actually binds (it stands at 47,3 ms / 15,8 % today, so
  the headroom is real).
- **Reported, not vetoing:** the far-tail (rich / cap profile) composed figure is measured at the
  chosen bound and **compared to 316,7 / 381,5 ms**. If it exceeds them, that widening goes to Klas
  — see escalation 1. A far-tail state that is already an accepted exception **may not veto a modal
  product failure**; it may only be re-granted or not.

**P4. The bound is the MINIMUM of three independently derived ceilings** — (a) the largest
**measured** point satisfying P3's binding ceiling, (b) whatever `security-auditor` derives from
Art. 5(1)(c), (c) the largest point not widening Klas-beviljande 4, unless Klas widens it.
**Interpolating between measured points is forbidden** — the grid is the instrument, and a value
between two measurements is a choice. A minimum over derived ceilings is a derivation; a preference
among them would not be.

**P5. The result lands in a new dated report plus an ADR 0139 amendment**, and
`CompanyWatchMaterialisationOptionsTests`' literal pin moves in the same commit as the constant.

## D2 — Decision **[BOUND]**: yes, an anchor number is admitted at N=1, narrowly

Part-3 D1 rested on three grounds and **all three genuinely lapse at N=1**: one predicate cannot
overlap itself; the single criterion's own `buildCriterionAdsHref` **is** the destination; and the
"aggregate" over one row is that row's own state. *"A finding whose premise is revoked does not hold
— and that applies to my own rulings or it is not a rule."* I apply it to myself here. The
prohibition on a summed anchor at **N ≥ 2 stands undiminished** and is not reopened.

The admission is conditional, and every condition is binding:

- **(i) It is not an aggregate; it is the row's own number relocated.** That is the entire ground of
  the admission, and it bounds it: N=1 only.
- **(ii) It REPLACES the row's number, never joins it.** The session's own reading is correct — an
  anchor number beside an identical row number is #1707 defect (a) one level up.
- **(iii) Same component, same DTO fields.** If the number moves into the anchor, it moves by
  *placing* `CriterionAdLines`' rendering there — never by `CriteriaSummary` reading `items[0].ads`
  and formatting it itself. Two renderers for one number is the drift class ADR 0139's *"Båda
  ytorna läser samma källa"* exists to close.
- **(iv) The anchor position must carry ALL FOUR states honestly, or the admission lapses.** If the
  chosen form can only carry a number, the block silently acquires two structures — one for watches
  with numbers, one for watches without — and the state Klas is in **today** is the one that falls
  out of the pattern. A form that only works once #1706 is fixed is not a form.
- **(v) If the anchor absorbs the row, it absorbs the LABEL too.** "1 bevakat företag" needs no
  identity because the sibling's watches are many and unnamed; a criterion's label is what makes its
  number's scope legible, and it is the only thing distinguishing this block from its neighbour.
- **(vi) `criteria-summary.test.tsx` ~118 is NOT edited.** It pins N=2 and an N=1 anchor cannot make
  it fail. Add beside it: an N=1 test, and a pin that **the number appears exactly once** on the
  surface — that pin is #1707 (a)'s acceptance made mechanical.

**[ADVISORY] — which of the two admissible forms.** Both satisfy #1707. The smaller one is
probably right and it is worth naming precisely: `CriterionAdLines`' `inList` flag already
discriminates *"is this one of many?"* — read its own docblock (*"the advice would repeat verbatim
per row, so the caller collects it and states it once"*). **At N=1 it cannot repeat**, so
`design-reviewer`'s Major 2 binding — which was argued from repetition — does not reach N=1 at all,
and the block advice simply suppresses. That fixes (a) with one condition and an existing, tested
branch. ⚠ If that route is taken, the flag's **name must be corrected to what it decides**
(one-of-many), not left saying "which page am I on" — a flag whose name states a different fact
from the one it switches on is the next drift. What I do **not** grant is fixing (a) by adding a
second copy of the refusal or a second computation of a number.

**[NOT DECIDED HERE]** Whether the block should actually take the anchor-number shape is
`design-reviewer`'s — she owns the form and bound the current split. I admit the number; I do not
require it.

### For `design-reviewer` — which states become common, which rare

Klas's *"helt konstig ut"* is **mostly the gate defect, not the form**: the block shows no numbers
at all only because the watch is refused. #1707's own warning applies to itself.

| State | Today (bound 1000) | After the re-derivation | Design consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tooBroad` (shared refusal) | **common** — 13,3 % of company mass, 57,2 % of Stockholm's, 3 of 5 dev criteria | **stays real, changes meaning** — a 9-code x 26-kommun criterion is 27 027 companies and is refused at any affordable bound; a single cell mostly is not | Do not design it away. It becomes the *genuinely broad* state it was always meant to be |
| ad number + link | uncommon | **the modal state** | This is what the block should be optimised for |
| **counted zero** | already common and easy to miss | **stays common** | Only 6 920 of 743 654 active register companies carry an active ad (**0,93 %**), so a narrow watch's ordinary honest answer is *zero* — not an edge case |
| `notMaterialised` | rare, transient (at most about one tick since klausul (ii)) | unchanged | Reachable right after creating a watch, which is a moment users see. Keep honest, do not optimise |
| `notAssessed` (matching `null`) | profile-dependent, not N-dependent | unchanged | `SetupCallout` already owns the nudge on this page |
| `countUnavailable` | rare | unchanged | Must survive any reshaping |
| **N = 1 itself** | Klas's state, and plausibly modal for a new account (`MaxPerUser` = 20 is the ceiling, not the shape) | unchanged | N=1 deserves to be designed, not treated as degenerate |

**[ADVISORY] One more thing she should see, and it is not mine to grade.** `deriveDisplayLabel`
names the **division** covering a leaf, with no "m.fl." when only one division is covered — so a
watch on the single leaf `62100` renders *"Dataprogrammering, datakonsultverksamhet o.d."*,

to two readers. A user cannot tell from `/oversikt` whether their own watch is narrow or broad, and
"välj färre branscher" reads absurd beneath a label that already looks like one industry. Its
grading belongs to whichever charter reports it; it must **not** expand #1706's scope.
