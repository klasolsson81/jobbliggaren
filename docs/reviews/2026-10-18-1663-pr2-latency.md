## The fixture

- One user at `CompanyWatchCriterion.MaxPerUser` (20) criteria, each `RecordScan(Scanned)` through the domain.
- Each criterion holds **2 500 unseen hits on Active ads**, plus **500 hits on Archived ads** that the next night's purge
  has not yet removed. No acknowledgement. 60 000 hit rows, 50 000 of them unseen: 20 × 2 500 is the pre-fixed gate
  `every_user_within_50000` that ADR 0175 D12 read true at 2026-10-18T18:25Z.
- **The unseen hits are spread over the whole Active range.** Criterion *k* (0–19, by id) holds a hit on every 17th Active
  ad starting at the *k*-th (42 500 ÷ 2 500 = 17), wrapping. Criteria 0–16 hold disjoint sets that together cover all
  42 500 Active ads; criteria 17–19 wrap onto the sets of criteria 0–2. That is 7 500 shared (criterion, ad) pairs, the
  minimum 20 × 2 500 − 42 500 allows. The 500 Archived ads are shared by all 20.
- `job_ads`: **43 000 rows**, 42 500 of them Active — at least the 42 302 Active ads D12 measured on the box — with wide
  descriptions (~1,8 kB each), bulk-inserted in the ingest's shape. Their facets are real taxonomy concept ids: the
  occupation group from the profile's 391 groups, a municipality from all 290 together with its own län, and an
  employment type from the 8 of Klass 2.
- The taxonomy is seeded by `TaxonomySnapshotSeeder`, the writer production runs at start; then `VACUUM (ANALYZE)` on
  the three tables.
- Nothing caps a criterion's hits except its member companies' Active ads (D12), so 2 500 per criterion is the gate,
  not a bound.

**What the fixture does not reproduce.**
- The hit table holds this one user only.
- `job_ads` holds 500 non-Active rows; production's non-Active share was not measured.

## The profile

security-auditor S-5's ceiling profile: **391 SSYK occupation groups, all 21 län, all 290 kommuner**, no employment type
stated. The 391 are the first 391 of the taxonomy's 400 groups by concept id, and every ad's group is one of them. It is
stored as the user's `MatchPreferences` through `JobSeeker.Register` and `UpdateMatchPreferences`, and built on every
call by the real `MatchProfileBuilder` over the real `TaxonomyReadModel`, so the profile build is inside the timed call.
The class asserts the built profile's three counts and that at least one graded row matches.

The page reads one criterion at its cap (`ListNewCriterionAdsQueryHandler.MaxRows` = 100, read as 101) and grades the
window through the real `PerUserJobAdSearchQuery`. 5 warm-up calls, then 40 timed calls per series; nearest-rank
percentiles.

## The count statement's plan

`EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)` of the statement the handler sent, captured from EF's command event in the same run and
re-run with its own parameters:

- **One aggregate:** a single `HashAggregate` (group key `company_watch_criterion_id`, 20 groups) over the `Hash Join` of
  `job_ads` (`Index Only Scan using ix_job_ads_status_published_at_id`, `status = 'Active'`, 42 500 rows) with the
  hits ⋈ criteria (60 000 rows), giving 50 000 rows into the aggregate.
- Above it a `Subquery Scan` hashed into a `Hash Left Join` on the owner's 20 criteria (`Seq Scan`), then
  `Sort` on `created_at DESC`.
- **No `SubPlan`; every node runs `loops=1`.**
- Execution 34,1 ms, planning 0,3 ms; `shared hit=1533`, no reads.

## Verdict against ADR 0045 class (a)

**Both reads are inside 300 ms p95**: the count at 33,2 ms, the page at 9,2 ms. No STOPP under AGENTS.md §2.5.

⚠ **What the figures are not.** They time the handler alone, called directly: not the Mediator behaviours,
serialisation, HTTP, the network, or Next's render. The fixture runs on a developer machine, not the box. So each figure
is a floor on the request a user waits for, never its ceiling.

## The rate-limit derivation from the same run (security-auditor S-5, CTO D-11)

The rule S-5 set: sustained ≤ ⌊1 000 ms ÷ p95⌋ per minute (the house anchor of ~1 s of database time per minute per
account from one route); burst ≤ 20, expressible under the TokenBucket truncation rule; the seen POST equal to the page.
Each policy's `PermitLimit` is min(20, ⌊1 000 ÷ p95⌋) and its `SegmentsPerWindow` equal to it (CTO routing item 13;
count-form ruling item 1). Both p95s are under 50 ms, so both reach the cap of 20.

| Policy | p95 | ⌊1 000 ÷ p95⌋ | Set | DB time/min |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `CriterionNewAdCounts` | 33,2 ms | 30/min | burst 20, 20/min: `PermitLimit = 20`, `WindowSeconds = 60`, `SegmentsPerWindow = 20` | ≈ 0,66 s |
| `CriterionNewAds` | 9,2 ms | 108/min | burst 20, 20/min: 20/60/20 | ≈ 0,18 s |
| `CriterionNewAdsSeen` | — | — | equal to `CriterionNewAds` | one conditional indexed update |

All three refill 1 token per 3 s; none truncates. The derivation is written out in each option's docblock in
`RateLimitingOptions.cs`, and `RateLimitingOptionsTests` pins the limiter options
`RateLimitingExtensions.PerUserTokenBucketOptions` builds from each. security-auditor signs the numbers at PR review
(BLOCKING, the file's convention).
