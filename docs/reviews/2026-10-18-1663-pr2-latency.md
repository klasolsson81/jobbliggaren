## The ceiling

- One user at `CompanyWatchCriterion.MaxPerUser` (20) criteria, each `RecordScan(Scanned)` through the domain.
- Each criterion holds **2 000 unseen hits on Active ads**, plus **500 hits on Archived ads** that the next night's purge
  has not yet removed. No acknowledgement. 50 000 hit rows in all.
- `job_ads`: **40 000 rows** with wide descriptions (~1,8 kB each), bulk-inserted in the ingest's shape; then
  `VACUUM (ANALYZE)` on the three tables.
- Nothing caps a criterion's hits except its member companies' Active ads (ADR 0175 D12), so 2 000 per criterion is a
  stated order of magnitude, not a bound.
- The page reads one criterion at its cap (`ListNewCriterionAdsQueryHandler.MaxRows` = 100, read as 101) and grades the
  window through the real `PerUserJobAdSearchQuery` against a rich profile: 50 occupation groups, 21 regions,
  100 municipalities, 8 employment types (the 2026-09-07 report's shape). The profile build is stubbed and not timed.
- 5 warm-up calls, then 40 timed calls per series; nearest-rank percentiles.

|---|---|---|
| `GET /new-ad-counts`, 20 criteria at the ceiling | 22,2 ms | **23,7 ms** |

Seeding took 16 s; the run 29,5 s.

## Verdict against ADR 0045 class (a)

**Both reads are inside 300 ms p95**, by more than an order of magnitude. No STOPP under AGENTS.md §2.5.

⚠ **What the figures are not.** They time the handler alone, called directly: not the Mediator behaviours,
serialisation, HTTP, the network, or Next's render. The fixture runs on a developer machine, not the box. So each figure
is a floor on the request a user waits for, never its ceiling. The margin to 300 ms is wide enough that this does not
change the verdict; it does change how the figures may be used, which is why the rate-limit arithmetic below leaves room
for a multiple of them.

## The rate-limit derivation from the same run (security-auditor S-5, CTO D-11)

The rule S-5 set: sustained ≤ ⌊1 000 ms ÷ p95⌋ per minute (the house anchor of ~1 s of database time per minute per
account from one route); burst ≤ 20, expressible under the TokenBucket truncation rule; the seen POST equal to the page.

| Policy | p95 | ⌊1 000 ÷ p95⌋ | Chosen | DB time/min at the ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `CriterionNewAdCounts` | 23,7 ms | 42/min | burst 20, 20/min | ≈ 0,47 s |
| `CriterionNewAds` | 10,8 ms | 92/min | burst 20, 20/min | ≈ 0,22 s |
| `CriterionNewAdsSeen` | — | — | equal to `CriterionNewAds` | one conditional indexed update |

All three: `PermitLimit = 20`, `WindowSeconds = 60`, `SegmentsPerWindow = 20` → 1 token per 3 s, no truncation. The
derivation is written out in each option's docblock in `RateLimitingOptions.cs` and pinned in
`RateLimitingOptionsTests`. security-auditor signs the numbers at PR review (BLOCKING, the file's convention).
