## The fixture (registered in the harness docblock before the first run; never changed after a result)

- **The shared Branschbevakning layer** (`CriterionHitStockSeed`): `job_ads` 50 500 rows, **50 000 Active** (S-2 read 45 260 on
  the box on 2026-11-20, `…-pr3-s2-reading.md`; the fixture was raised before any harness ran), wide descriptions (~1,8 kB),
  real taxonomy facets. One user at 20 criteria, each `RecordScan(Scanned)`. Criterion *k* holds a hit on every 20th Active ad
  from the *k*-th, so at 50 000 Active ads **every Active ad holds exactly one hit** (20 × 2 500 = 50 000); plus one hit on each
  of the 500 Archived ads per criterion, waiting for the purge (10 000). 60 000 hit rows, 50 000 of them new. Stamps follow ad
  order.
- **The bell's layers** (`NotificationFeedLatencyMeasurement`): the user has PR 2's ceiling profile (391 occupation groups, 21
  regions, 290 municipalities) and the background-match consent. A match row (`UserJobAdMatch.Create`) on each of the
  **newest 5 000** Active ads, and a follow hit (`FollowedCompanyAdHit.Create`), all under one watch on the company those ads
  carry, on each of the **newest 15 000**. So the match source holds 5 000 Branschbevakning ads and the follow source 15 000,
  of which the match source holds the newest 5 000 first; the follow source counts 10 000, its own `MaxCountedHits`, so its
  grade path counts at the bound it declares.
- **Why the held ads are the newest:** each lower source's window reads newest first and skips what a higher source holds, so
  this arrangement makes every window walk past every held hit before it reaches one it shows. It is the costliest arrangement
  of the same rows, and an account produces it by following the companies its Branschbevakning reaches.
- **What it does not reproduce:** the tables hold this one user; production's other users, its non-Active share and its
  archived-hit stock between purges were not measured.

## The protocol

- **The read** (rule (ii)): the handler `GetMyNotificationFeedQueryHandler` with the real `MatchProfileBuilder` and
  `PerUserJobAdSearchQuery`, 5 warm-up calls then 40 timed calls per pass, nearest-rank p50/p95. Four passes per case: the
  follow source on its common path (the watch without a filter), on its grade path ("endast matchade"), then both again in
  reverse order. Two cases: the base case, and the shortfall case after 600 clicks through `MarkNotificationReadCommandHandler`
  on the newest rows each source shows (every window then holds fewer unread rows than its count). p is the highest p95 of
  the eight passes. Each case's statements are captured once, counted (13 and 16, asserted) and EXPLAINed (ANALYZE, BUFFERS).
- **"Markera alla"** (the architect's protocol): autovacuum off on `company_watch_criterion_ad_hits`, ANALYZE while the stock
  is unread; the statement captured without being sent; its first call under EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS) inside BEGIN …
  ROLLBACK, then for real, timed once; then a timed loop of calls that write nothing straight away, and again after
  VACUUM (ANALYZE), each with its plan. Rule: ⌊1000 ÷ the higher of the two no-op p95s⌋ ≥ 60, with the expected plan.
- **PR 2's reads** (`CriterionNewAdsLatencyMeasurement`, unchanged code on the raised fixture): the count route, the page at
  its cap graded with the ceiling profile, a reverse-order control pass, and the page with no stated occupation.

### "Markera alla" on the Branschbevakning source

- ANALYZE with 59 400 of the user's 60 000 hits unread (600 read by the shortfall case's clicks; 10 000 of the unread are on Archived
  ads, which `NewHits` never counts).
- **First call for real: 1 440,1 ms, 49 400 hits marked. ADR 0045 class (c), 400 ms: exceeded.** Under EXPLAIN in a rolled-back
  transaction: a hash semi join of the target's sequential scan against all of `NewHits`.
- **Calls that write nothing:** p50 138,2 / p95 **181,4 ms** straight away; p50 106,5 / p95 133,7 ms after VACUUM (ANALYZE).
- **Rule: ⌊1000 ÷ 181,4⌋ = 5 < 60. The rule fails, and so does the plan check:** neither no-op plan is the expected target range
  on `ix_company_watch_criterion_ad_hits_user_created_at` with the `EXISTS` never executed. Straight away the planner builds all of
  `NewHits` (a HashAggregate over 50 000 rows) and probes the target by primary key; after VACUUM it builds a Hash Right Semi Join
  with all of `NewHits` as the probe side. The 10 000 unread hits on Archived ads survive the target's flag filter on every call.
  Under the CTO's registered order the form goes back to dotnet-architect before any number is priced.
