# Jobbliggaren — LinkedIn film 2 "Väntläge" (v4)

Format: 1080×1350 (4:5), 30 fps, 44.0 s. Real-time 3D (Three.js/WebGL, rendered frame by frame) with
typography overlays. Built for sound-off viewing: the airport announcements are on-screen MEDDELANDE cards.

Metaphor: a job application is a paper plane. Without control it ends up circling in a storm called
"VI ÅTERKOMMER". Jobbliggaren is the air-traffic control for your job search.

fictional demo data. Product facts as in the app: four match grades, follow-up queue with recalculated
waiting time, watch notifications tagged "Bevakar" (no grade), rule-based CV review with 43 criteria (beta)
whose verdicts quote the CV; the profile-length rule's limit is 100 words.
